03 / faq

          
Common questions.

        

        

          

            How do we get started?
            
Send a message through the contact form. We set up a short call to understand what you need, then share a written scope with timeline and cost before any work begins.

          

          

            Can you take over software someone else built?
            
Yes. We start with a review of the existing code and infrastructure, tell you plainly what state it is in, and then take on fixes, upgrades and ongoing maintenance.

          

          

            Who owns the code?
            
You do. Source code, documentation and account credentials are handed over to you. There is no lock-in.

          

          

            How do you handle security?
            
It is part of design and development from the first day: least-privilege access, reviewed dependencies, encrypted connections and secrets kept out of code. Security reviews of existing systems are also available on their own.

          

          

            Do you work with clients outside Mumbai?
            
Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere, with regular updates and a single point of contact.
