Dost means friend. Dost Initiative is a place to talk things through, whether the question is about your life, your studies or your career.

Start a conversation How we can help

01 / how we can help

Guidance for life and for work.

Bring whatever is on your mind. We start by listening.

Personal conversations

A calm space to talk about what you are going through: stress, change, relationships, or a decision you keep circling.

Career guidance

Help thinking through a career choice, a job change or what to do next, based on your strengths and situation.

Students and early careers

Support with choosing a course, handling study pressure and taking the first steps into working life.

02 / how it works

Three simple steps.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Send us a short message. You do not need to explain everything at this stage.

  2. 02

    First conversation

    We talk, understand what you are looking for and agree on how we can help.

  3. 03

    Keep going

    Continue for as long as it is useful to you, at a pace you choose.

Listen first

You are heard before any advice is offered.

No judgement

Whatever you bring, you are met with respect.

At your pace

You decide how much to share and how far to go.

Please note: Dost Initiative is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services straight away.
